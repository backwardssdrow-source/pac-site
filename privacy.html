Privacy

A plain-language privacy note for PAC.

Pragmatic Artistry Collective is built around care, consent, and human legibility. This page explains how this public site handles information at its current stage.

This Website

What this site collects

This static website does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information directly on the page. If that changes, this note will be updated.

Contact

When you reach out

Contact links open an email to PAC. Anything you choose to send by email is received through that email exchange and used to respond to your inquiry.

Booking

Third-party services

Booking is not open yet. When booking tools are added, any connected scheduling or payment services may handle information according to their own policies.

Mirror

The Mirror pilot

Mirror is a separate pilot experience. It is designed to support self-understanding, not diagnosis or treatment, and its access and data practices may be described separately as the pilot develops.

Principle

No selling personal information

PAC does not sell personal information. The work is oriented toward protecting context, not extracting it.
