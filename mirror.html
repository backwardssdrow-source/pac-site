What Mirror Is

Mirror doesn’t tell you who you are.

It helps you become more understandable to yourself.

Through reflection, gentle pattern recognition, and transparent explanations, Mirror helps you notice what restores you, what drains you, and what keeps repeating.

Mirror isn’t trying to remember everything about you. It’s helping you decide what is worth remembering.

Mirror was built on a simple belief: your life already contains extraordinary understanding. Our job isn’t to generate it. It’s to help you recover it, protect it, and return it to you.
