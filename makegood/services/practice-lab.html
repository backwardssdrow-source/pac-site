Practice Lab

A facilitated session that helps ERG leaders or sponsors put ideas into everyday practice.

Choose one track: ERG Leader Practice or Sponsor Practice. One cohort, one session and practical commitments to take back to work.

What is included

  • Brief client intake and an adapted MakeGood worksheet or toolkit.
  • One two-hour virtual session for up to 20 participants.
  • Facilitated practice around real situations.
  • 30-day action commitments and a short follow-up note.

Scope and boundaries

One track and one cohort. This is not the three-session Presence Lab, a portfolio diagnostic, governance build or formal conflict resolution. Additional customization is separately scoped.
