What is included

  • A short intake and one 60-minute online session with one MakeGood coach.
  • Time to work through a decision or rehearse a conversation.
  • Next steps written down together during the session.
  • One session at a time, with no package or monthly commitment. We confirm the fee and session details before booking.

Scope and limits

No formal report, custom research, interviews with colleagues, extensive document review, or ongoing messaging. Coaching is not therapy, crisis support, an investigation, or legal advice. We agree confidentiality and any employer reporting beforehand.
