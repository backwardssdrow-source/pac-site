Coaching

Coaching & Thought Partnership

A focused conversation for a question about work, leadership, communication, boundaries or a transition.

Bring the situation you are in. You do not need a polished brief or an organizational budget. We agree whether coaching, experience-based advice or a blend would be most useful.

What is included

  • Short intake and one 60-minute virtual session with one MakeGood coach.
  • Space to reflect, rehearse a conversation or work through a decision.
  • Practical next steps captured together during the session.
  • Book once or return when useful. No package, retainer or introductory sales call is required.

Scope and boundaries

No formal report, custom research, stakeholder interviews, extensive document review or ongoing messaging. Coaching is not therapy, crisis support, an investigation or legal advice. Confidentiality and any employer reporting are agreed beforehand.
