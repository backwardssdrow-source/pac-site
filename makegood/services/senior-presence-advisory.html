Leadership Advisory

Ongoing advice for leadership decisions, conversations, and working relationships.

Bring a decision, a difficult conversation, or feedback you need to act on. Regular sessions and feedback between meetings help you prepare, try an approach, and review what happened.

What is included

  • Two 60-minute advisory sessions each month.
  • One 30-minute preparation session each month.
  • Up to four short document or audio reviews each month. Each covers up to two pages or five minutes of audio. Replies are normally within two business days.
  • A short written summary of the advice and next steps, plus an end-of-term summary.
