Three workshops to improve how your leadership team works together.

For an existing leadership or sponsor team working on communication, decisions, inclusion, disagreements, or trust. We help connect shared goals with changes to how the group works together.

What is included

  • An intake with the sponsor and up to four 30-minute interviews.
  • A written summary of team feedback and three two-hour guided workshops.
  • Written team agreements, guidelines for communication and decision-making, and a 90-day implementation plan.
  • One round of revisions based on combined feedback and a 60-minute follow-up around 45 days after the last workshop.

Scope and limits

One team of up to 12 participants. Complex active conflict, larger teams, or additional groups need a different scope. This is not therapy, an investigation, or formal conflict resolution.
