Presence Studio

Presence Lab

Help a leadership team put shared values into everyday practice.

Shared practice for an intact leadership or sponsor team around trust, clarity, authority, participation, conflict and repair.

What is included

  • Sponsor intake and up to four 30-minute interviews.
  • A Team Presence Brief and three two-hour facilitated labs.
  • Written team agreements, communication and decision protocols, and a 90-day implementation plan.
  • One consolidated revision and a 60-minute follow-up around 45 days after the final lab.

Scope and boundaries

One team of up to 12 participants. Complex active conflict, larger teams or additional cohorts need a different scope. This is not therapy, an investigation or formal conflict resolution.
