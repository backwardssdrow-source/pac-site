About MakeGood.

LT and Travis founded MakeGood Co. to help people make decisions at work, lead teams, and run employee resource group programs.

We look at the support, decision-making authority, and workload people have—not just what policies say. Then we help change what is getting in the way.

Our mission

We help people and organizations put their values into practice through stronger relationships and more human ways of working.

Our vision

A world where organizations are built to be truly human: life-giving, relational, and restorative.

Meet the founders.

Co-Founder

LT Peterson

LT Peterson is an organizational advisor, investigator, and leadership strategist who helps people build more human ways of working together.

At Uber, she developed global strategy and led governance across 15 employee resource groups in four regions.

At MakeGood, LT brings an investigator’s curiosity and an artist’s imagination to coaching and organizational advisory. She helps leaders navigate difficult decisions, strengthen employee communities, and turn complexity into practical next steps.

Her approach pairs candor with care, grounded in a conviction: people should not have to become less human to succeed at work.
