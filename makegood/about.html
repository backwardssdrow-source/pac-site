About MakeGood

People. Culture.
Possibilities.

We work with the people building employee communities, leading teams, and finding their own way at work.

We look at how people experience an organization as human beings—not just as employees, leaders, or job titles. That means considering both the people affected by decisions and the people responsible for making them.

Our mission

We help people and organizations put their values into practice through stronger relationships and more human ways of working.

Our vision

A world where organizations are built to be truly human: life-giving, relational, and restorative.

People

Start with how people experience the organization: what they need, what they carry, and where they have room to act.

Culture

Examine relationships, resources, and decisions—then improve how people work together and advocate for themselves and others.

Possibilities

Find shared concerns and goals, even when they first seem unrelated, and turn them into practical changes.

The people behind MakeGood

Meet the founders.

Together, LT and Travis bring 39 years of combined professional experience helping people lead, strengthening communities, and building better ways of working.

LT

Co-Founder

LT is an organizational advisor, investigator, and leadership strategist who helps people build better ways of working together.

Her background spans internal audit, investigations, and global people programs. At Uber, she developed strategy and led governance across 15 employee resource groups in four regions.

At MakeGood, she brings an investigator’s curiosity and an artist’s imagination, helping leaders navigate difficult decisions and strengthen employee communities. She is also founder and Chief Artist of Pragmatic Artistry Collective, developing research, tools, and creative practices that explore lived experience and the conditions people need to flourish.

Her approach pairs candor with care: people should not have to become less human to succeed at work.

Travis

Co-Founder

Travis is a community builder and leadership advisor who helps organizations turn belonging into everyday practice.

Travis’s experience spans 12 years directing a collegiate spirit program and a decade at Uber, in roles including Executive Assistant and Global Head of Employee Resource Groups. At Uber, Travis co-founded the Pride ERG, directed global Pride campaigns, and helped lead the evolution of its global employee-community programs.

At MakeGood, Travis brings a connector’s instinct and experience leading through change—helping organizations listen to their people, strengthen community leadership, and connect employee needs with business priorities.

The work is grounded in a belief that people have something valuable to contribute—and organizations get stronger when they make room for it.
