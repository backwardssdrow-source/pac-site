About MakeGood

People. Culture.
Possibilities.

We work with the people building employee communities, leading teams, and finding their own way at work.

We look at how people experience an organization as human beings—not just as employees, leaders, or job titles. That means considering both the people affected by decisions and the people responsible for making them.

Our mission

We help people and organizations put their values into practice through stronger relationships and more human ways of working.

Our vision

A world where organizations are built to be truly human: life-giving, relational, and restorative.

People

Start with how people experience the organization: what they need, what they carry, and where they have room to act.

Culture

Examine relationships, resources, and decisions—then improve how people work together and advocate for themselves and others.

Possibilities

Find shared concerns and goals, even when they first seem unrelated, and turn them into practical changes.

The people behind MakeGood

Meet the founders.

Together, we bring experience turning employee voice into clearer decisions, practical support for community leaders, and stronger organizational relationships.

LT

Co-Founder

LT brings an investigator’s curiosity and a strategist’s eye to organizational challenges. Her background spans internal audit, investigations, and global people programs.

At Uber, she helped shape strategy and strengthen governance across 15 employee resource groups in four regions. She worked on clearer roles, decision pathways, leadership development, and the resources ERG leaders need to do their work well.

At MakeGood, LT helps clients identify what is getting in the way, make difficult decisions with better evidence, and turn insights into practical plans. She is also founder and Chief Artist of Pragmatic Artistry Collective, exploring the conditions people need to flourish.

Her approach pairs candor with care: people should not have to become less human to succeed at work.

Travis

Co-Founder

Travis brings deep experience building communities, leading programs, and helping people find a voice in organizations.

His background includes 12 years directing a collegiate spirit program and a decade at Uber, including roles as Executive Assistant and Global Head of Employee Resource Groups. He co-founded Uber’s Pride ERG, directed global Pride campaigns, and helped guide the evolution of its employee-community programs.

At MakeGood, Travis helps clients strengthen sponsor and leader relationships, support community leaders, and connect employees’ experiences with organizational decisions. His approach combines relationship-building with practical guidance for leading through change.

He believes organizations are stronger when people have meaningful room to contribute.
