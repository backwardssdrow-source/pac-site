About MakeGood Co.

People. Culture.
Practice.

MakeGood Co. is an independent company founded by LT and Travis. We bring coaching, ERG advisory and leadership development together to help people and organizations build better ways of working.

We start with the conditions people actually experience: the support they have, the decisions they can make, and what is expected of them. Our work turns those experiences into clearer choices, workable structures and practical next steps.

Our mission

We help build better people and better systems — so our partners can produce better outcomes for the people and communities they serve.

Our vision

A world where organizations are built to be truly human: life-giving, relational, and restorative.
