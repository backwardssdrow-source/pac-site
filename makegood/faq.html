Good questions

A little clarity
before we begin.

No mystery around the starting point, the scope or what happens next.

Do I need a company budget to work with you?

No. Coaching & Thought Partnership is for individuals. You can book one conversation without buying a package or committing to a larger engagement. The fee is confirmed before booking.

What do the founding fees include?

Each published fee covers the defined base scope shown in the service details. Additional participants, listening, customization or on-site delivery may require a separate scope. We confirm the final agreement before work begins.

How is delivery arranged?

We agree the delivery format, location and schedule before the work begins. Any travel or venue costs are pre-approved and separately scoped.

Do we have to buy a diagnostic first?

No. A diagnostic can be useful when the system or problem is unclear, but there is no required service ladder. The right starting point depends on what you already know and the decision you need to make.

How do you handle confidentiality?

We agree confidentiality and reporting boundaries before the work begins. For stakeholder listening and individual presence work, raw notes and attributed comments are not handed over to sponsors. Any employer reporting is defined in advance.

What happens when we start a conversation?

We clarify the question, timing, participants and scope. When there is a fit, a written agreement confirms the work and fee. The conversation does not require you to purchase an engagement.

How long does founding pricing apply?

The first three core contracts and first five paid Decision Sessions or Practice Labs are eligible, with one founding core contract per organization. Coaching is separate. Retainer founding fees cover the initial three-month term only. Availability is confirmed, not implied by this page.

Future quotes and renewals may change after review. Existing signed fees and deliverables remain unchanged unless both parties agree a change. Candid feedback is welcome; testimonials, logo use and case studies are optional and require separate permission. No positive review is required.

What are the payment terms?

Decision Sessions are paid in full to reserve. Other fixed-fee projects below $25,000: 50% at signature and 50% before final delivery. Custom projects of $25,000 or more: 40% / 30% / 30%. Retainers are billed monthly in advance. Exceptions are agreed in writing.

What happens if the scope changes?

Added interviews, regions, languages, documents, sessions, revisions, deliverables or access need written scope and fee approval. Travel is optional, pre-approved and separate.

Confidentiality, reporting, data handling, scheduling and cancellation terms are agreed before work. Clients receive agreed internal-use rights to custom deliverables; pre-existing methods and templates remain with their respective owners.
