Clear commitments

What the founding fee means

A defined scope. First three core contracts and first five paid Decision Sessions or Practice Labs; one founding core contract per organization. Coaching is separate. Retainer founding fees cover the initial three-month term only. Availability is confirmed, not implied by this page.

No automatic price lock. Future quotes and renewals may change after review. Existing signed fees and deliverables remain unchanged unless both parties agree a change.

Feedback, not endorsements. Candid feedback is welcome. Testimonials, logo use and case studies are optional and require separate permission. No positive review is required.

Payment. Decision Sessions are paid in full to reserve. Other fixed-fee projects below $25,000: 50% at signature and 50% before final delivery. Custom projects of $25,000 or more: 40% / 30% / 30%. Retainers are billed monthly in advance. Exceptions are agreed in writing.

Privacy and ownership. Confidentiality, reporting, data handling, scheduling and cancellation terms are agreed before work. No raw interview notes are supplied. Clients receive agreed internal-use rights to custom deliverables; pre-existing methods and templates remain with their respective owners.

Changes. Added interviews, regions, languages, documents, sessions, revisions, deliverables or access require written scope and fee approval. Travel is optional, pre-approved and separate.
